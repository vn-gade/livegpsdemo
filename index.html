⚠️ The Problem

Why Urban Transportation Needs Solving

Without real-time data, cities suffer from inefficient traffic management and unpredictable public transport — costing millions in time and fuel every day.

⏰
45 min

Time Loss

Average commuters lose over 45 minutes daily stuck in congestion, translating to hundreds of hours per year and significant productivity loss for individuals and businesses alike.

⛽
30%

Fuel Wastage

Stop-and-go traffic caused by poor congestion management wastes up to 30% of vehicle fuel, directly increasing operational costs for fleet operators and individual commuters.

🌿
23%

Pollution Increase

Urban transport contributes 23% of total city CO₂ emissions. Congestion amplifies this figure — idling vehicles emit up to 3× more pollutants per kilometer than vehicles in free flow.

How It Works

1
📡

GPS Data Collection

Bus drivers use the Driver View app on their mobile phones. The browser's built-in navigator.geolocation API captures real-time latitude, longitude, speed, and accuracy data every few seconds.

2
🔢

Congestion Scoring

Road segments are scored 0–100 based on traffic density, time-of-day patterns, and historical data. Scores are classified: Low (0–33), Medium (34–66), and High (67–100) with color-coded visualization.

3
🗺️

Live Map & Analytics

Leaflet.js renders interactive maps with colored polylines for road segments. Chart.js powers the analytics dashboard with hourly congestion trends, weekly patterns, and distribution breakdowns.

4
👁️

Faculty Monitoring

Faculty staff access a dedicated dashboard showing all active buses in real time. Cross-tab sync via localStorage events simulates live data. Full deployment uses Firebase Realtime Database.
